What is cardinality?
    Cardinality is expressed from the perspective of the OUTGOING entity.
    It has a minimum and maximum.
    If minimum is missing, the relationship is optional.
    If maximum is missing, there's no limit to the number of related outgoing entities.
    Minimim cannot be greater than maximum.
    Maximum must be greater than 0 if it is present.
    Minimum must be greater than 0 if it is present.
